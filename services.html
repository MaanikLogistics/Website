Our Services

Logistics solutions for every cargo type, every lane, every business size.

Air & Sea Freight

To deliver every client shipment on time, we partner with the world's largest air carriers and shipping lines. Whether your cargo needs the speed of an air freighter or the capacity of an ocean container, we'll route it through the most cost-effective and reliable lane available.

  • Direct relationships with major airlines and ocean carriers worldwide
  • FCL (Full Container Load) and LCL (Less than Container Load) options
  • Door-to-door, port-to-port, and door-to-port service models
  • Hazardous, oversized and temperature-controlled cargo handling
  • End-to-end customs clearance and documentation

E-Commerce Delivery Solution

Our association with all the world's major postal and express delivery providers means our delivery solutions cover every major location your customers live in. We help online retailers and marketplaces deliver consistently — at competitive rates that protect your margins.

  • Integration with all major postal networks and last-mile partners
  • Marketplace-ready labels, manifests, and customs paperwork
  • Return management and reverse logistics workflows
  • Custom packaging, pick & pack, and kitting at origin
  • Branded tracking experience for your customers

Overseas Warehousing & Return Management

Keep inventory close to your customers with our overseas warehousing network. From bulk import to last-mile fulfilment, our facilities operate as your forward stocking points — slashing transit times, duties, and customer complaints.

  • Forward stocking locations in key international hubs
  • Inventory management with real-time stock visibility
  • Returns processing, inspection, and restocking
  • Cross-border consolidation and deconsolidation
  • Bonded and non-bonded storage options

Express Delivery

For your time-sensitive shipments, we offer delivery solutions through all the major express delivery companies. Documents, samples, replacement parts, or urgent freight — we'll get it there in the shortest possible time, with priority handling and live tracking the whole way.

  • Same-day and next-day delivery in key markets
  • Priority handling at customs and gateway hubs
  • Live tracking with proactive exception alerts
  • Dedicated agent on call for time-critical shipments
  • SLA-backed delivery commitments

E-Commerce Export Advisory

Expanding internationally is more than just shipping boxes — it's compliance, taxation, channel strategy, and customer experience. Our advisory team helps Indian sellers and brands successfully launch and scale on global e-commerce platforms.

  • Market entry analysis and channel selection (Amazon, eBay, Walmart, etc.)
  • Export documentation, IEC, AD code, and compliance setup
  • HS code classification and duty optimisation
  • Pricing, FBA prep, and inventory planning
  • Ongoing operational support and account management

Personal Baggage

Moving abroad, returning home, or just sending personal belongings across borders? We offer safe, door-to-door delivery for unaccompanied personal baggage, with packing support and end-to-end tracking from collection to drop-off.

  • Door-to-door pickup and delivery worldwide
  • Packing materials and on-site packing assistance available
  • Insurance options for high-value items
  • Customs documentation handled on your behalf
  • Dedicated coordinator for every move
