Who We Are

Your gateway for global trade solutions

Maanik Logistics is an international sourcing and logistics company connecting buyers worldwide with reliable Indian manufacturers and suppliers. We help businesses discover high-quality products from India while simplifying sourcing, procurement, and international transportation.

India is one of the world's most dynamic manufacturing ecosystems — but navigating it from abroad is complex. Languages, time zones, supplier credibility, quality consistency, export paperwork and freight logistics all stand between an overseas buyer and the right product at the right price. Maanik exists to remove every one of those frictions.
