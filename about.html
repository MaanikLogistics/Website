Our Story

Worldwide logistics support since 2023

Maanik Logistics was founded by promoters with their 20+ years of experience in the express and freight industry. What started as a vision for faster, more transparent international logistics is today a global operation trusted by businesses and individuals across continents.

We're run by a team of 200+ professionals located in India and abroad — people who understand the core mechanics of express delivery and the critical importance of meeting every deadline. Being a service industry, we recognise our people are our most valuable assets, and we nurture our staff to attain their fullest potential by creating an environment that promotes both personal and professional development.
